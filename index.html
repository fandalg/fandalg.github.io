About Me

My leadership spans from launch planning to portfolio optimization, enabling sustainable access and patient impact in complex markets.

  • Developed AI-driven omnichannel strategies tailored to stakeholders across 27 countries
  • Led two pan-European launches across diverse ecosystems, delivering best-in-class results and setting benchmarks for execution excellence
  • Proven ability to align global brand direction with local market realities to overcome access hurdles
  • Trusted partner for executive teams seeking market clarity, digital transformation, and launch excellence
